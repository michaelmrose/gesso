* Scope and acceptance decision

This is a *DOCUMENT-ONLY* checkpoint after v1380. It changes no runtime
operation, checker, certificate gate, authorization rule, HTMX behavior, or
public API. It reviews the actual v1371–v1376 source in the supplied Gesso
repository snapshot and the original v1378, v1379, and v1380 installation TAR
payloads. v1377 repaired a separate test-corpus integrity expectation.

*Decision: THE BOUNDED PHYSICAL-EXECUTION DIAGNOSTIC FOUNDATION IS ACCEPTED AS
ENGINEERING EVIDENCE; NO PHYSICAL REFINEMENT OBLIGATION IS DISCHARGED.*

The observed path now connects four *different kinds* of evidence:

1. Source-relative issued operation and writer obligations (v1366–v1371).
2. Process-local preparation and physical-outcome hook events (v1373).
3. Caller-recorded low-level transaction operations (v1375).
4. Post-attempt physical readback (v1378) and supplied before/after comparison
   over the admitted, finite target set (v1380).

The evidence can detect inconsistencies between these sources. It does not
authenticate their origin, the transaction-internal basis, the complete
physical write set, or the identity of the execution causing a later read.
Successful correlation or matching rows *must not* be promoted into a proof
certificate, admission, or transaction authority.

This checkpoint covers Gesso's single-node server-side assurance development.
HumanHelp production assurance, multi-node execution, browser/HTMX/SSE
refinement, and universal checker-kernel soundness are outside its established
claims. They remain part of the broader R4 goal.

* Evidence inventory and precise boundaries

** A. Source-relative writer and issued-executor accounting

The v1366/v1368 bridge rechecks a declared operation catalog against an issued
application, its handler identities, and a *supplied* host enumeration of
observed entry points. It derives per-operation abstract read/write/frame
requirements and nine per-writer obligations. This is useful source-relative
accounting. It cannot prove that the supplied enumeration covers hidden
jobs, raw XTDB callers, initializers, alternate routes, or deployed binaries.
It also does not prove that an identifier-matched concrete handler implements
its independent abstract transition relation.

** B. One-attempt hook observations (v1373)

Namespace: =gesso.assurance.proof.transaction-bound-evidence-acquisition=.

=start-acquisition!= accepts the original checked writer source, operation
identifier, and canonical command binding. The returned =:observe!= function
receives a =:prepared= event and a =:physical-outcome= event on the specific
resolved-operation invocation; =:complete!= can return a report only after
one eligible, definitively classified attempt. Missing, duplicate,
reordered, inconsistent, or observer-poisoned events fail closed. A genuine
indeterminate outcome does not become a definitive completed report.

The observer is installed through an injected context hook, not an
independently authenticated XTDB event stream. A report is replayable and
copyable. It retains =:hook-origin-authenticated? false= and
=:transaction-id-authenticated? false=.

** C. Canonical lowering versus caller-recorded submission (v1375)

Namespace: =gesso.assurance.proof.transaction-submission-correspondence=.

=audit-recorded-submission!= replays upstream acquisition and lowers the
captured admitted plan using =gesso.model.tx/prepare=. It compares the entire
ordered operation vector with caller-supplied =:recorded-tx-ops=. It can
reject an altered, incomplete or reordered vector and accepts at most 128
recorded operations. The comparison is about *what those two vectors say*;
it is not an authenticated observation of the bytes submitted to XTDB.

** D. Bounded post-attempt state (v1378–v1379)

Namespace: =gesso.assurance.proof.transaction-outcome-state-provenance=.

=audit-outcome-state!= rechecks the original v1375 evidence, attempt ID,
operation ID, binding, command-record identity, observed outcome, and
reported receipt. It compares expected *fields* of each admitted target
against ordered supplied =:post-rows=. =capture-xtdb-poststate!= can issue
real =xt/q= SELECT queries against a *caller-supplied* XTDB node, for at
most 64 distinct plan targets including the command record. The node's
identity, query basis, and relation to the transaction are not attested.
Neither a matching row nor a matching recorded receipt establishes causality.

The report differentiates =:observed-committed-projection-agrees=,
=:observed-committed-projection-diverges=, and
=:rejected-outcome-no-causal-attribution=. Its agreement predicate covers
expected fields only: unexpected physical fields may be omitted from that
particular comparison.

The real-XTDB test also revealed an exact representation mismatch in the
stored command record's timestamp fields: a sub-microsecond fraction of an
expected =java.time.Instant= was not preserved in the observed value. A
=ZonedDateTime= readback is compared to =Instant= by their *exact instants*,
not by printed text or silent truncation. For that controlled case, the
mismatch remains visible; the test does not assert a general storage-precision
theorem for every XTDB write or type.

** E. Declared write footprint and bounded frame (v1380)

Namespace: =gesso.assurance.proof.physical-write-footprint-observation=.

=audit-declared-write-frame!= rechecks the entire v1378 source/report chain
and derives admitted command targets, declared before/after documents,
declared guard/assertion counts, and the ordered caller-recorded =:put-docs=
count. It supports at most 64 unique declared targets. The restricted shape
requires one supported =:put-docs= per admitted command, plus supported
ASSERT statements; other recorded operation shapes fail closed.

The v1380 input supplies pre-attempt document rows, explicitly marked
=:caller-supplied-unattested=. It compares them with declared prestates,
then compares whole *supplied target documents* after the attempt against
the declared results. It reports missing fields, additional post-fields,
field-delta disagreement, and differing target indices without returning
sensitive domain documents in the report or typed errors.

This strengthens the observed *declared target set* relative to v1378's
expected-fields-only projection. It still cannot detect writes to entirely
unlisted documents, authenticate a transaction-internal prestate, exclude
concurrent writers, or prove that this attempt caused the observed poststate.
For a rejected attempt, the result remains a rejected-attempt diagnostic,
not a successful frame or commit proof.

* Physical correspondence theorem still required

Let =alpha= project an authenticated concrete XTDB state onto the typed
abstract state, =Init= denote admitted initialization, =T_o= denote an
admitted guarded abstract operation, and =C_o= a concrete execution of its
checked implementation. The desired bridge has at least the following
premises for the claimed domain and deployment:

#+begin_example
  (1) Every real initialization is represented by an admitted base state.
  (2) Every writer of protected physical state is mediated and classified.
  (3) The request's typed input, principal, and permissions are trustworthy.
  (4) The transaction-internal prestate and guard checks are coherent.
  (5) Each committed C_o either refines T_o under alpha or is allowed
      abstract stuttering; rejected/unknown outcomes do not forge success.
  (6) Physical writes and untouched frame fields are completely accounted for.
  (7) Concurrent histories satisfy the serial/atomic model's assumptions.
  (8) Source, proof evidence, checked handlers and deployed artifacts are bound.
  (9) The independent abstract semantics and preservation rules are sound.

  THEN, conditional on these premises, abstract invariant preservation
  transfers to the claimed concrete executions.
#+end_example

The v1380 observed path addresses small, diagnostic portions of (3)–(6)
under explicit, unauthenticated premises. It has *not* established those
premises. Finite Recife/TLC exploration of abstract interleavings and
successful embedded-XTDB tests cannot substitute for the missing universal
implementation/refinement obligations. Recife remains internal tooling;
application authors do not write Recife, Quint or TLA+ specifications.

* Prioritized next steps

** v1382 — Executor-controlled acquisition boundary

Design an acquisition boundary in which the observed operation vector and
outcome are collected by a controlled executor rather than supplied freely
by an external caller. First identify exactly which lower-level Clojure/XTDB
callsite is under Gesso's control and which facts XTDB genuinely exposes.
Preserve an explicit attacker/bypass model: instrumenting an optional callback
is not an attestation of every physical transaction. Do not claim trusted
origin until excluded bypasses and artifact identity are established.

** v1383 — Caller impersonation and observer bypass tests

Permanently test forged hook events, fake low-level operation vectors,
replaced receipts, stale reports and bypassed/disabled observers. Any
certificate-like promotion must fail closed. Preserve redaction of domain
values, command bindings and receipts from diagnostic errors.

** v1384–v1387 — Typed binding and transaction-internal prestate

Trace a checked request to typed input and inspect the actual XTDB facilities
for transaction-relative snapshot/basis and guard observation. Only claim
facts supported by a genuine trusted execution interface. A caller-supplied
=before= map or postcommit SELECT cannot establish transaction-internal
prestate. Capture failure/unavailability remains a typed open obligation.

** v1388 onward — Guard, authorization and physical effect fidelity

Tie source-derived guards and =tx/prepare= lowering to the actual execution
boundary; then validate conflict outcomes, complete write mediation, and
physical frame preservation. Distinguish normal transient failures from a
known rejection and from =:commit-unknown=. Use Recife for appropriate finite
abstract interleavings, with separate real-XTDB checks and universal-proof
obligations. Later stages address proof-kernel soundness, certificate policy,
and portable JVM/Node/browser correspondence, without introducing Quint or
requiring downstream formal-language authoring.

* Operational guardrails and v1381 handover

- Preserve the exact nine v1368 obligation identifiers and their original
  evidence labels. No broad =:verified true= substitute is acceptable.
- =:commit-unknown= must never be recast as committed from matching readback.
  Current v1373 completion does not support an indeterminate attempt.
- Never compare imprecise timestamps by silently rounding/truncating them.
  Establish any storage-time abstraction rule independently and test it.
- A target-document projection, even with all its fields, is not a complete
  write footprint unless target enumeration and physical write mediation
  are separately justified.
- Neither a matching source fingerprint nor self-replayed checker result
  authenticates the physical host, executable, transaction, or deployment.
- Retain =:admission :not-admitted=, =:certificate :not-issued=, and
  =:authority :none= (and =:transaction-authority :none= wherever returned).
- Preserve the separation between historical test evidence and tests
  actually completed in a new revision; do not count timed-out gates.
- Next numbered revision: *v1382*; it should be a complete file replacement
  in a two-member =ins=-compatible TAR, with independent focused testing.
